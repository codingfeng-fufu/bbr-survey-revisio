# Final review notes

Contribution: the narrative survey retains its version/scenario synthesis and conditional framework; no new controller or universally validated selection rule is claimed.
Clarity: search provenance is condensed in the manuscript and documented in a supplement; the abstract and conclusion agree with the experimental scope.
Experimental support: raw receiver data for 340 runs independently reproduce all per-run rate/share/Jain values and the 68 condition means. E1, E2, E3 and artificial-loss settings and results are visible in the manuscript.
Evaluation completeness: RTT and queue snapshots have narrower observation windows than the primary goodput measurements; warmed-window sensitivity and E3 physical queue corrections are stated. No full-run zero-loss claim survives.
Mechanism soundness: BDP identity does not infer allocation; ECN absence does not remove loss feedback; Mathis is scoped to Reno-like behavior; source-specific version defaults are distinguished from experimental-build identity.

The targeted revision adds a 165-reference identity/role register, explicit analytical assessment and retrospective transfer errors against a constant baseline, including failure cases and a requirement sensitivity grid. No retrospective analysis is labelled preregistered. The search does not support an exhaustive systematic-review claim; this claim was withdrawn. The response explains this limitation and the available search records. This is a bounded final revision, not a completed full-text literature-screening project.
