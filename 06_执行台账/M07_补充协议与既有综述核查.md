## 3. 需要加入的比较字段

对BDP-Veno、OVeno以及本文已有BBR条目统一提取：

`study_id, report_id, algorithm, base_protocol, bottleneck_bandwidth, RTT_or_delay_definition, BDP_used_as_input, bandwidth_estimation_method, delay_signal, loss_signal, ECN_used, target_cwnd_or_inflight, queue_model, flow_count, scenario, simulator_or_platform, baselines, throughput_metric, delay_metric, fairness_metric, retransmission_metric, result_location, limitations`

特别区分：

1. **BDP作为控制输入**与**BBR用BtlBw×RTprop建立工作模型**不是同一个机制结论。
2. BDP-Veno/OVeno的性能比较若来自单一仿真配置，只能支持该配置下的结果，不能直接用于本文三维框架阈值。
3. 没有公平性指标的研究可以用于机制/吞吐背景，但不能支持公平性结论。
4. 未报告队列、RTT或流数的研究标 `NR`，不能用来填补场景分类表。

## 4. 直接可写入相关工作段的暂定表述

> Beyond BBR variants, recent BDP-oriented TCP studies provide a useful comparison point. BDP-Veno incorporates bottleneck BDP information into a Veno-based controller, whereas OVeno focuses on optimizing Veno parameters through stochastic approximation. These designs share an interest in using path information to regulate the congestion window, but they are not interchangeable with BBR's delivery-rate and propagation-delay measurement model. Their reported results should therefore be compared by control signal, queue/loss assumptions, baseline, and evaluation metric rather than by the BDP label alone.

这段只能在OVeno书目信息和两项研究全文核实后进入正文。当前保留为草稿，不作为已完成的正文修改。
