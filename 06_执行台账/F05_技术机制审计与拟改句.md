**拟替换表注：**

> ProbeRTT's target in BBRv2/v3 is bounded by the larger of 4 MSS and BDP/2 in the relevant implementation state. The values of `cwnd_gain` and related pacing gains are state- and version-dependent; the table reports only implementation-verified values. When ECN marking is unavailable, ECN-derived feedback is absent, but BBRv2/v3 retain loss-driven bound updates and state-control logic. Therefore, the absence of ECN alone does not imply reversion to BBRv1.

## 2. RTT公平性段落

原文把 `BDP_i = BtlBw_i × RTprop_i` 后直接推成“长RTT必然获得更多带宽”，并使用“geometric fact, not a bug”。这超过了恒等式和现有证据能支持的范围。

**拟替换 Section 5 开头段落：**

> Intra-protocol RTT unfairness is associated with the interaction between each flow's delivery-rate and propagation-delay estimates, the probing schedule, and the shared queue. The identity `BDP_i = BtlBw_i × RTprop_i` determines the in-flight amount corresponding to a measured operating point; by itself, it does not determine the bandwidth allocation among competing flows. A larger in-flight amount may be necessary to sustain the same throughput on a longer path. The observed throughput share therefore depends on RTT ratio, queue depth, version, queue management, flow count, and startup or probing dynamics. We use the identity to define a testable mechanism hypothesis, not as a sufficient proof that a long-RTT flow must obtain a larger share.

删除或替换：`This is a geometric fact, not a bug.`

**图注改法：** Figure 5/6 只能写“observed RTT-dependent share under the stated configuration”或等价限定语；不能写成“long-RTT flows necessarily capture larger shares”。

## 3. 框架判定条件

当前正文仍有以下需要同步收紧的内容：

1. `suitable`必须拆成吞吐、时延、公平性和重传四个输出；综合等级若保留，必须写出阈值/权重/冲突处理。

3. 多流场景说明BDP的参照RTT、绝对队列字节数和每流RTT；不能只写倍数。
